## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Gas giant rings have structure and take the planet's shadow

A ringed giant's rings SHALL show ringlets and gaps, SHALL be lit by the sun
on their sunward face and glow when seen against it, and SHALL be darkened
where the planet stands between them and the sun. The rings' shadow SHALL
fall on the planet's cloud tops.

#### Scenario: Looking down on the rings
- **WHEN** the player looks at Brontes from above its ring plane
- **THEN** the rings show ringlets and a dark gap, and the part behind the planet from the sun is in shadow

### Requirement: Gas giants have atmospheres

A gas giant SHALL be drawn with a haze shell and limb haze in its own tint
on its day side, warmer along the day/night line, and none on its night side.

#### Scenario: The limb
- **WHEN** the player sees a gas giant's lit limb
- **THEN** a soft glow of its atmosphere rings the edge of the disc
