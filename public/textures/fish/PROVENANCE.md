# Fish icons (16x16)

- `minnow`, `reef`, `eel`, `ray`, `serpent`: copied from this repo's Rust
  tree, `tenebris-rs/assets/textures/items/fish_*.png`
  (generator `tenebris-rs/tools/gen-fish-sprites.py`, MIT).
- `silverfin`, `perch`, `deepback`: copied from RubenTipparach/Pale-Blue-Dot
  `assets/items/fish/` (drawn by its `tools/gen_item_icons.py`), along with
  the fishing rules in `src/player/Fishing.ts`.
- `tetra`, `catfish`, `leaf_pike` (Verdance) and `peat_eel`, `gourami`,
  `violet_gar` (Mirewood): drawn by this repo's `scripts/gen-item-icons.py`
  (`fish_icon`, PICO-8 colours matching their swimming models in
  `src/config/FishRosters.ts`).
- `sprat` (Sequoia's glimmer sprat, openspec underwater-life): drawn by the
  same `fish_icon` in `scripts/gen-item-icons.py`.
- `pearl_mullet`, `kelp_eel`, `abyss_grouper` (Thalassa, openspec
  space-exploration): drawn by the same `fish_icon` in `scripts/gen-item-icons.py`.
